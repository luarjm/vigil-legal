FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Why does the app need location access?

VIGIL uses your GPS coordinates to generate the hexagonal sector map around your real location and detect when you visit sectors.

Why does the app need HealthKit access?

Your step count is the main game resource — every 1,000 steps generates fuel for your shelter. Sleep and workout data provide secondary bonuses.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. All data stays on your device. VIGIL has no backend, no accounts and no analytics.

The map is not loading. What should I do?

Make sure location permissions are set to "Always" in Settings → Privacy → Location Services → VIGIL. The map generates once a GPS fix is obtained.

My steps are not being counted.

Go to Settings → Privacy → Health → VIGIL and make sure read access is granted for Steps.
